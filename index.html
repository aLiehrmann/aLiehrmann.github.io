Research achievements

My research focuses on the development and application of statistical models, algorithms, and methods for the analysis and interpretation of high-throughput biological data.

Computational Statistics

  • Statistical inference: changepoint detection (segmentation), penalized likelihood
  • Algorithms: dynamic programming, functional pruning

Biostatistics/Bioinformatics for Molecular Biology

  • Models: segmentation, generalized linear models, phylogenetic comparative methods
  • Data: RNA-Seq, Ribo-Seq, ChIP-Seq, RIP-Seq
  • Package development: R/C++/python

1 - A. Liehrmann, E. Delannoy, B. Castandet and G. Rigaill. 2023. Link | Software
DiffSegR: an RNA-Seq data driven method for differential expression analysis using changepoint detection. NAR Genomics and Bioinformatics

  • DiffSegR is the segmentation method I developed during my PhD. From RNA-Seq data alone, it recovers the full set of annotated transcriptomic regulations in the Arabidopsis chloroplast, something no competing method achieves (best at 80%), while finding new ones. Its genericity is demonstrated through its application to sequencing data [4, 5], and its conclusions hold at nuclear genome scale [2].

Showcasing my expertise in applying segmentation methods to sequencing data (& others)

2 - V. Brunaud, G. Rigaill, and A. Liehrmann. manuscript in prep.
DiffSegR2: genome-scale data-driven differential expression analysis

  • We show that the method DiffSegR remains robust at the scale of whole nuclear genomes.

3 - M. Abakarova, M. I. Freiberger, A. Liehrmann, M. Rera and É. Laine. 2026. Link
Proteome-wide prediction of the functional impact of missense variants with ProteoCast. Nature Communications

  • I applied segmentation to predicted protein mutational landscapes to identify putative binding and regulatory sites in unstructured regions, outperforming competing methods.

4 - K. Baudry, S. Skiada, C. Burstein, V. Liveanu et al. (incl. A. Liehrmann). 2026. Link
The GT1 domain of RNase J ensures RNA quality control through dsRNA binding in Arabidopsis plastids. Nucleic Acids Research

  • Using DiffSegR on RNA-Seq data from multiple RNase J mutants in Arabidopsis, I showed that mutants accumulate high levels of dsRNA in the chloroplast.

5 - H. C. Tran, V. Schmitt, S. Lama, C. Wang et al. (incl. A. Liehrmann). 2023. Link
An mTRAN-mRNA interaction mediates mitochondrial translation initiation in plants. Science

  • Using DiffSegR on RIP-Seq data, I showed that Arabidopsis mTRAN factors bind preferentially to 5′ regions of mitochondrial mRNAs.

6 - A. Liehrmann, G. Rigaill and T. D. Hocking. 2021. Link
Increased peak detection accuracy in over-dispersed ChIP-seq data with supervised segmentation models. BMC Bioinformatics

  • I showed that supervised segmentation of ChIP-Seq data yields higher-quality histone modification peak tracks than bioinformatics heuristics.

Showcasing my expertise in fine-grained modeling of transcriptomic and proteomic data
